# Product Requirement Document (PRD)

**Product Name:** CivicLens AI

**Document Version:** 3.1 (Complete Specification with End-to-End Technology Stack)

**Target Clients:** Municipal Corporations (e.g., GVMC), Public Transit Boards, Traffic Enforcement Wings

---

## 1. System Vision & Problem Statement

### 1.1 The Operational Bottleneck

* **In-Bin IoT Sensors:** Degrade within months from acidic leachate, rodent damage, and physical impacts during compactor emptying ($₹1.2\text{ Cr}+ \text{ CapEx per 5,000 bins}$).
* **Citizen Reporting Apps:** Suffer from friction; citizens do not routinely photograph or touch dirty bins to scan QR stickers ($<3\% \text{ compliance}$).
* **Central Grievance Portals:** Add bureaucratic delay with 24–72 hour resolution cycles for routine overflows.

### 1.2 The CivicLens Solution

CivicLens turns existing municipal vehicles (sweepers, waste tippers, transit buses) into autonomous civic auditors. A refurbished budget smartphone running on-device edge AI captures road conditions, classifies multiple municipal defects, checks local ward boundaries via Point-in-Polygon (PiP) GIS, and automatically pushes incident tickets to departmental Telegram channels in under 5 seconds.

### 3.2 Electrical & Operating Logic

1. **Ignition-Linked Power:**
* **ACC ON (Vehicle Running):** 5V DC flows to phone; system wakes up or boots via `fastboot oem off-mode-charge 0`, acquires a partial CPU wakelock, and starts the detection loop.
* **ACC OFF (Engine Stopped):** USB power cuts; `ACTION_POWER_DISCONNECTED` intent stops the camera session, flushes unsent queues, and puts the device to sleep.


2. **Thermal & Battery Protection:**
* **Display Off:** The app runs as an Android Foreground Service with the screen set to 0% brightness or blacked out, cutting internal operating heat by 40%.
* **Thermal Throttling:**
* $< 40^\circ\text{C}$: Standard sampling (1 frame every 1.5 seconds).
* $40^\circ\text{C} - 45^\circ\text{C}$: Throttled mode (1 frame every 4.0 seconds).
* $> 46^\circ\text{C}$: Camera inference halts; device sends a low-power telemetry alert and sleeps for 5 minutes.


* **Stationary Detection Check:** If GNSS speed is $< 3\text{ km/h}$ (e.g., waiting at traffic signals), camera inference pauses to prevent duplicate frame processing.

### 4.1 Edge Inference Pipeline

* **Vision Model:** YOLOv8n / YOLOv11n INT8 quantized ($\le 6\text{ MB}$).
* **Execution Engine:** TFLite Runtime or ExecuTorch using mobile GPU/NPU delegates.
* **Core Model Classes:**
* `0: garbage_overflow` (Confidence threshold $\ge 0.70$)
* `1: road_pothole` (Confidence threshold $\ge 0.75$)
* `2: open_manhole` (Confidence threshold $\ge 0.85$ - High Priority)
* `3: external_accident` (Confidence threshold $\ge 0.75$)
* `4: illegal_banner` (Confidence threshold $\ge 0.75$)


* **On-Device SLM Validator:**
* Runs **only upon a positive vision detection** to validate context and generate plain-English summaries (e.g., *"Significant roadside waste overflow on lane shoulder, partially obstructing footpath"*).
* Avoids thermal load by staying dormant during routine driving frames.



### 4.2 Offline-First Storage Engine

* **Local Database Schema (`incident_outbox`):**
* `incident_id` (UUID, Primary Key)
* `timestamp` (Unix Epoch Milliseconds)
* `latitude`, `longitude` (Double, 6 decimal points)
* `defect_type` (Enum: `GARBAGE`, `POTHOLE`, `MANHOLE`, `ACCIDENT`, `OVERSPEED`)
* `confidence` (Float)
* `slm_summary` (Text)
* `image_path` (Internal storage URI)
* `sync_status` (`PENDING`, `SYNCED`, `FAILED`)


* **Data Lifecycle:** Images are compressed to 40–50 KB WebP files. Upon receiving an `HTTP 200 OK` from the server, the local image file is erased to avoid consuming device flash memory.

## 5. Backend Processing & Multi-Department Routing

### 5.1 Ingestion API Specification

* **Route:** `POST /api/v1/telemetry/incident`
* **Payload Type:** `multipart/form-data`
* **Form Fields:** `device_id`, `timestamp`, `lat`, `lon`, `defect_type`, `confidence`, `slm_summary`, `evidence_image` (Binary).

### 5.2 Spatial Deduplication & Ward Matching

1. **Deduplication Engine (PostGIS):**
* Computes spatial proximity: `ST_DWithin(new_geom, existing_geom, 15.0)` for all unresolved tickets of the same `defect_type` logged within the last 12 hours.
* **Match Found:** Updates recurrence count (`sighting_count += 1`), logs latest vehicle sighting, and suppresses new notifications to prevent alert fatigue.
* **No Match:** Creates a new master incident record.


2. **Point-in-Polygon (PiP) Ward Resolver:**
* Maps coordinates using `ST_Contains(ward_polygon.geom, incident_point.geom)`.
* Automatically resolves and tags `ward_id`, `zone_id`, and assigned departmental field units.



### 5.3 Multi-Department Dispatch Matrix

| Incident Type | Trigger Source | Target Department | Channel & SLA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Vehicle Crash / Rollover** | IMU Deceleration Spike ($\ge 4.5g$) | Traffic Police & Emergency Medical (108) | Direct Webhook / SMS Dispatch ($< 5\text{ seconds}$) |
| **Open Manhole / Missing Cover** | Vision Model ($\ge 0.85$ conf) | Water Supply & Sewerage Board | Emergency Dispatch Bot ($< 15\text{ minutes}$) |
| **Waste Overflow / Blackspot** | Vision Model ($\ge 0.70$ conf) | Public Health & Sanitation | Ward Sanitary Inspector Telegram Bot ($< 2\text{ hours}$) |
| **Pothole / Road Cave-in** | Vision Model ($\ge 0.75$ conf) | Roads & PWD Civil Wing | Municipal Work Order Queue ($48\text{ hours}$) |
| **Fleet Driver Overspeeding** | Fused GNSS vs Speed Limits | Transit / Fleet Depot Management | Daily Driver Performance Audit |

### 6.1 Design Constraints

* **Anti-Overwhelm Principle:** Replaces raw telemetry tables with prioritized card feeds and two-click dispatch actions.

* **Two-Pane Operational Workflow:** Officers select an alert card on the left pane and review the map, verified photo proof, and dispatch actions on the right pane.
